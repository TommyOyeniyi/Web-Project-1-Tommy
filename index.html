All About LeBron James

Welcome to the page about LeBron!

You may ask, "Who is LeBron?"

LeBron Raymone James is a 41-year-old American professional basketball player for the Los Angeles Lakers and the NBA's all-time leading scorer. He is a four-time NBA Champion who won his first NBA title back in 2012. LeBron James was born in Akron, Ohio. He lives with his wife, Savannah James, and their younger children in Los Angeles, California.
